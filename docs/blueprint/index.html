miovra.eu · Wireframe & copy blueprint

Crafted elegance, laid out page by page

This is the plan for the new miovra.eu, built on the page flow and components of The India Craft House and dressed in Miovra's own two-tone identity. It covers three sample pages: the Homepage, a Collection page with filters, and a Product page. Each section shows the layout, how colour and UI carry the sense of luxury, and the exact copy. No front-end code gets written until you approve it.

What we take from the reference

The India Craft House runs on a calm, editorial shop template: an announcement band, a slim header, a full-width slideshow, alternating story and category blocks, square-cornered buttons, a serif display face over a geometric sans, and a three-column footer. Below, each reference component is mapped to its Miovra version. Miovra has five collections rather than hundreds of products, so we keep the structure and trim the volume.

Design system: two tones, nothing more

Every option uses exactly two colours, a light ground and a dark ink. Borders and hairlines are only transparent tints of the dark tone. All the other colour on the site comes from the photographs: the block-print reds, teals and brass. Click a palette to re-colour this whole blueprint, mock-ups included.

Typography

Display · Cormorant Garamond 500–600

Crafted Elegance

I come from many Indias.

Body & UI · Jost 400 / 500

Jost is a near twin of Futura PT, the body face on the reference site. Cormorant echoes the high-contrast capitals of the MIOVRA wordmark and sits a weight lighter than the reference's bold serif, which reads as quieter and more luxurious.

Announcement bar & header H1

Layout

  • 36 px announcement band, full width, one centred line (it can rotate up to three messages).
  • Header in three zones: search left, lotus and wordmark centred, wishlist and bag right. The menu row sits beneath it, centred.

  • Mobile: hamburger left, logo centre, bag right. The drawer slides in from the left.

Colour & UI

  • Band: dark ground, light 12 px caps, tracking 0.2 em.
  • Header: light ground, dark ink, 1 px hairline (dark at 14%) underneath. No shadow.
  • Active menu item: 1 px dark underline. Hover underline draws left to right in 300 ms.

Full Our Story page copy (from your profile, punctuation tidied only)

I come from many Indias.

My roots are in Uttar Pradesh, I grew up in Pune, and I married into a family from Uttarakhand. Over the years, family and friends from many other states made my world a gathering of languages, festivals, foods, colours and customs.

For most of my life I worked in the corporate world. But something in me always leaned toward art and culture, toward the hands that make things and the stories that live inside them.

Then I moved to Sweden.

I settled into a new country and a new culture, and I found a quiet life I came to love. But I missed the vibrancy of India: the colours, the festivals, the sense that tradition is never far from your hands. So I started exploring Indian art in my free time, and the more I looked, the more I saw how little of it is known in modern India, leave aside Europe.

So I began Miovra. People said no one cares who is making it or where it comes from; it wouldn't work; it was too common, too boring a business. I listened, and then I followed my heart anyway. I strongly believe India's art forms deserve a wider audience, and the people who create them deserve more recognition and respect.

Everything at Miovra is made using simple, time-honoured methods, by artisans who learned their craft from the generations before them. Each piece carries their patience and care.

Our collection is small because it is carefully selected and curated with the artisans. We tell you how each piece was made, and we hope it brings a little warmth, colour and meaning to your home.

Welcome to Miovra. Crafted elegance, with heart.

Filters, sort & product grid C3

Layout

  • Toolbar: Filter toggle on the left, result count in the middle, Sort on the right, between hairlines. It sticks under the header while scrolling.

  • Product card: 4:5 photo (a second photo appears on hover), wishlist heart, Quick Add bar on hover. Below it, an eyebrow line “Craft • Region” (the reference's “State • Craft”), the name and the price.

Colour & UI

  • Checkboxes are square 1 px outlines that fill dark. The slider is a 1 px line with outlined handles.

  • Names in Cormorant 20 px, prices in Jost with tabular figures so they line up.

Build plan & open questions

Once you approve, the build runs in these phases. Everything lives in the Miovra GitHub repository and runs on your Mac first. Deployment to your Oracle Cloud (OCI) server only happens after you approve the finished site.

Proposed stack

Front endNext.js (React), server-rendered for speed and Google visibility; images resized automatically
Shop & adminMedusa, an open-source store engine: products, stock, orders and an admin panel where you add pieces yourself
DatabasePostgreSQL
PaymentsStripe: cards, Klarna, Swish, Apple Pay / Google Pay. Test mode until launch
HostingOCI Compute VM, Docker Compose, Caddy for automatic HTTPS
Domainmiovra.eu currently points to a Shopify store showing “Store unavailable”. DNS moves to OCI at launch

  6. Catalogue. A first list of products (name, collection, craft, region, size, price, photos). A spreadsheet like your profile file is ideal.
